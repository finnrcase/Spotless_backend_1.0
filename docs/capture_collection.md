# Practical Westwood count and media collection

The existing [phone collector](phone_collection.md), [16-face development plan](../reports/collection/development_plan.json), archived NAIP annotation tool and separate product review gates remain distinct. Manual count intake/export and the historic 24-face image annotation flow already work. The extension adds bounded local photo/video metadata intake, duplicate-aware visit units, a blinded review queue, agreement statistics and recorded operator effort. It does not train a detector or promote its output.

```powershell
.\.venv\Scripts\la-parking.exe collection plan
.\.venv\Scripts\la-parking.exe collection metadata --input path/to/photo.jpg
.\.venv\Scripts\la-parking.exe collection import --input data/local_capture_manifest.jsonl
.\.venv\Scripts\la-parking.exe collection audit
# Open reports/collection/development_plan.html and review_queue.html locally.
.\.venv\Scripts\la-parking.exe collection review --input path/to/capture-reviews.jsonl
.\.venv\Scripts\la-parking.exe collection effort --input path/to/operator-effort.jsonl
.\.venv\Scripts\la-parking.exe collection audit
```

The first audit contains zero imported modern captures, zero reviewed count units and no recorded operator hours. Agreement, detector error/precision/recall and usable counts/hour are unavailable. The supplied NAIP JPEG and tiles are 2022-05-11 **date-only** images, with no independent current reference annotations or configured detector. Their existing feasibility findings are preserved; they are not current time-specific occupancy labels. No newly supplied modern imagery was available for measured detector results.

The development plan selects eight short physical blocks, both faces (16 faces), across inventory meter-link and no-meter-link strata. Parking type still requires reconnaissance. Within each stratum it mixes 75% uniform selection and 25% historical geometry/evidence-gap priority, using sequential weighted draws without replacement. It logs each **conditional draw probability**; marginal inclusion probabilities remain unknown, so do not use these numbers as survey inverse-probability weights. Prior geometry quality and missing evidence guide targeting, not held-out outcomes or retrospectively chosen model errors. The constrained short-block frame and purposive dates are not representative LA sampling.

All previously reserved unseen and known-future physical blocks are excluded. Eight separate sessions span morning/midday/evening November 1–3, with actual LA wall-time construction across DST. Each session follows one physical block, two sides four minutes apart at 0/15/30/60, for 64 planned snapshots. One operator cannot survey all 16 faces every 15 minutes; this schedule deliberately separates blocks. Use this bounded development plan rather than assuming the older 204-visit route can also be completed. Log actual times and skips; route links prefill stable IDs and coordinates are reference pins, not fabricated capture geotags.

`MediaCapture.schema.json` records stable capture/face/session/visit IDs, source, local file or manual-observation reference, capture time/date/precision, receipt, coordinate method, visible fraction, occlusion, occupied visible count, physical and verified/uncertain/unknown legal capacity, profile/stay, restriction evidence, confidence and privacy state. Files remain where supplied and outside Git; intake hashes bounded files (250 MB each, 200 records/batch), stores immutable metadata in ignored SQLite, and records actual local receipt. Exact-byte duplicates alias an existing sample unit. All adjacent frames from one source/face/visit are one unit even when hashes differ; repeats use different visit IDs. Reimporting an identical ID is idempotent; conflicting IDs fail the whole transaction. No camera filename/mtime is treated as a capture clock.

EXIF suggestions use only capture clock/offset, GPS and dimensions, with review required. A local clock without an offset remains unresolved. Video import needs a short clip/frame plus a sidecar with exact capture time, frame offset and camera geolocation; no unavailable decoder or GPS trajectory is invented. GPS/pin metadata must be checked against the actual face and side. Capture after receipt is rejected. Reserved physical blocks/sessions/periods are quarantined and excluded from development review; this does not authorize opening prior holdout images or observations.

The review queue hides earlier counts and CV candidates, while allowing a common visible-area mask to be shared. Choose a local image, draw visible-curb/occlusion rectangles, give the physical visible-scope ID, count manually, and verify privacy, clock and location independently. Retain raw visible fraction and unknown curb; do not scale visible counts to fill hidden areas. Two blinded independent human counts over the **same scope/masks** permit agreement assessment. Disagreements need a third documented human adjudication; no automatic averaging or convenient maximum. Repeated frames are equal-weighted by unique visit unit in agreement summaries. Complete visible count readiness still is not a legal-vacancy label; capacity/rules and direct suitability review remain separate. No detector proposal is automatically trusted.

Log operator intervals as JSONL with `effort_id`, anonymous `operator_code`, aware `start_utc`/`end_utc` and `activity` (`travel`, `capture`, `manual_count`, `review`). Same-operator overlapping intervals are rejected. Report usable complete-count visit units per **all recorded travel/capture/review operator hours**, plus raw effort coverage; self-reported time may be incomplete. Zero effort is unknown productivity, not infinite yield. Inter-rater count error is not detector accuracy or downstream forecast gain.

For the smallest useful capture batch:

1. Choose a generated development session and confirm the two face endpoints, public access, parking type, intended stay/profile and posted/temporary restrictions. Keep reserved sites/sessions untouched.
2. Set camera time with an explicit UTC offset and verify it before/after the walk; enable GPS or record a sidecar. Keep capture time, original receipt and review time distinct. Record video start plus each retained frame offset.
3. Capture while walking safely or from a passenger-operated ground camera. Frame curb occupancy rather than people or readable plates; review/redact identifiers locally before retaining a usable image. Do not record names, plates or personal travel histories. Keep private raw files out of Git/cloud publication.
4. At 0/15/30/60, take actual manual counts and a short overlapping image sequence of the same side, with signs/exclusions and visible/hidden curb documented. Watch actual arrivals/departures only during explicitly logged windows. Stable face/session/visit IDs link the modalities; multiple neighboring frames remain one observation unit.
5. Have two independent raters count a fixed visible scope without seeing predictions or each other's counts. Preserve disagreement, occlusion and ambiguous parked-versus-moving traffic. Record total operator and review time, not just shutter time.
6. Export manual counts, metadata and reviews separately. Assess agreement/visibility/cost before collecting hundreds of images. Use reviewed direct labels and the controlled A/B/C/D experiment for forecast value; no current detector score can be reported from absent modern reference imagery. Future drone imagery follows the separate simulation-only feasibility protocol, not an approved flight.
