# Modeling gate

The confirmed reporting and split requirements are implemented in `src/la_parking/evaluation.py` and documented in [evaluation_protocol.md](evaluation_protocol.md). The new executed pipeline and [measured report](../reports/modeling/results_report.md) now contain metered **sensor-panel proxy** scores, separate chronological/spatial/combined tests, trained baselines/statistical/boosted models, calibration and uncertainty. No unmetered or full-curb suitability scores exist. No fixed R² target applies; earlier rough ranges were speculative.

The foundation supports acquisition, spatial/temporal inspection, collection design and **experimental metered-only** label construction. It is not ready for a validated citywide suitability model.

1. **Direct evidence:** acquired monthly sensor events and last-known states cover a selective metered subset. There are zero verified unmetered occupancy labels. Do not infer unmetered labels from absence of a meter, citations, payments or nearby business counts.
2. **Truth versus policy:** event states are observations; any persistence interval is derived. Freshness caps are unvalidated assumptions, with 5/15/60-minute sensitivity outputs. Unknown state, stale state and no observation are different from vacant/occupied. We never use Spotless's 25-active-day rule and do not inherit its 24-hour persistence cutoff.
3. **Supply/rules:** citywide source streets and both sides exist, but most side geometry is approximate. Physical curb geometry is not legal capacity. Full sign content, eligibility, intersection/bus/loading exclusions and temporary rules are incomplete. Unknown capacity remains null.
4. **History:** current meter coordinates, rates, businesses and inventory cannot be assumed valid in September 2025 or August 2026. Create effective-date histories, detect sensor relocations and collect successive raw snapshots. NOAA station history found here does not overlap the acquired occupancy months; retain that gap.
5. **Evaluation:** establish independent prospectively collected face/time counts and suitability checks; use temporal backtesting with an embargo, sensor/face grouped splits and out-of-area holdouts. Compare time-of-week and recent-state baselines before more complex models. Report calibration, count error, abstention/coverage and error by metered/unmetered, neighborhood, lead time and requested duration.

Candidate next models, only after these gates: hierarchical binomial/beta-binomial available-space counts with known eligible capacity; calibrated gradient boosting for strong tabular baselines; state-space/hidden semi-Markov models for irregular sensor events, missingness and persistence. Spatial partial pooling can improve data efficiency but cannot demonstrate accuracy in unlabelled neighborhoods. A restriction engine must evaluate the entire parking interval and user permit/vehicle eligibility independently of the availability forecast.

Blocking acquisition issues are recorded in the registry: Census HTML response, incomplete historical weather, failed Metro cancellation service, stale sweeping/permit/calendar-sign tables, unresolved reuse terms for several ArcGIS/LARIAC layers. These are material data limitations rather than reasons to fabricate or bulk-add weak features.
