# Westwood public context continuation

Mechanisms: events may shift local parking demand; sweeping and temporary activity may remove eligible supply. The evidence gap is issue-time-known, geolocated, time-resolved context, not another substitute occupancy label. The smallest acquisition is bounded to the existing Westwood box, twelve UCLA details, at most 100 featured Village listings, and 2,000 features per City layer. No source is promoted into serving or a historical experiment.

Run `la-parking acquire-westwood-context`; cached raw requests are reused. Use `--refresh` deliberately for a new snapshot. [Coverage, fields, terms and receipts](../reports/westwood_context/coverage.json) and the [short source table](../reports/westwood_context/coverage.md) record the actual run. Raw responses and immutable normalized snapshots stay under ignored `data/westwood_context/`. No keys, purchases or public deployment are needed.

They remain raw wall times with UTC null. Provider-predicted recurring listings stay explicitly labeled, not confirmed attendance. No complete exact-time event feature is yet supported. Website/provider reuse terms apply; an open-data redistribution license was not verified. |

Keep sign review independent; do not apply a polygon restriction to every enclosed face. |

Encoded dates remain date-only until daily hour/timezone semantics are verified. Permit/reservation presence is not a verified legal closure, and incomplete legal coverage cannot become unrestricted parking. |

No independent exactly timed full-face label table was recovered. The existing Venice study is not substituted for Westwood, and neither report is current occupancy truth. |

An official Hammer event sample displays 12:30 PM while encoding `12:30Z`; unresolved timezone semantics prevent its use as an exact-time predictor. The older Socrata sweeping table remains a separate stale source; the new City application layer does not retroactively validate it. The City service's empty copyright/license metadata is not an assertion of a new redistribution license. Raw data remain local; reports publish provenance and factual coverage only.

`westwood_context.features_asof` accepts request ID, issue/arrival time and representative coordinates. It requires receipt by issue, a maximum 48-hour snapshot age and the bounded geography. It exposes route-polygon and nearby-reservation candidate counts, not legal capacity or actual closures; reservation proximity uses 15 metric metres. Timed-event counts require source coordinates and explicit offsets. Missing/unresolved feeds stay null, including historical requests made before these snapshots. Current source edits are not used as historical information-availability timestamps. No model is fit by this function.

The new sources add verified route/permit geometry, current calendar/listing metadata and a separate historic reference. They do **not** add occupancy labels, validated unmetered predictions, complete effective restrictions or a measured R² gain. The controlled prospective A/B/C/D experiment must decide whether any context helps on matched reviewed observations; new data count alone is not a promotion criterion.
